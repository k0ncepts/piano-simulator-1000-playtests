Pick a song and a paper size. Open PDF shows it in your browser; print it from there with the viewer's print button, or Ctrl+P (⌘P on a Mac). Download PDF saves a copy. If your browser opens the file instead of saving it, save or share it from the viewer, or print straight from there. US Letter is the usual paper in the US and Canada, A4 almost everywhere else.

With or without fingering? The finger numbers were generated too, and in dense passages some are drawn well away from their notes or on top of other marks. The versions without fingering have the same notes without the numbers, so they avoid that clutter, though not every crowded passage.
